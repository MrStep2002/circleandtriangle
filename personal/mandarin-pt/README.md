# Meu Mandarim (Portuguese, vocab only)

A Brazilian Portuguese copy of My Mandarin with just the Essentials vocabulary practice:
New Words, Review, Random, Choose groups, By category, Quick Match, Word Bank and Settings.

- Words, meanings, "sounds like" spellings and tips: `data/essentials.js`
- It keeps its own progress (separate storage name), so it never touches the English app,
  even on the same github.io site.
- After any change, update `VERSION` in `sw.js` (keep the `my-mandarin-pt-` start).
